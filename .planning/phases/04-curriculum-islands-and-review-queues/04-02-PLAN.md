# Plan 04-02: Reading And Tier Policy Regression Gates

## Objective

Strengthen curriculum validation around CI+1 reading coverage and staged complexity, especially early-tier avoidance of language islands and adult operating-life themes.

## Context

The project contract requires reading passages to stay within CI+1 math, language islands to remain gated until roughly 1000 known words, and work/health/money/admin pressure to remain at 2000+ tiers. Existing tests cover the happy path; this plan should add negative regression cases where the validator must reject bad content.

## Tasks

<task id="04-02-1" title="Add negative reading validation tests">
Add tests that prove readings fail validation when known-word coverage falls outside the allowed range, when too many new words are introduced in one sentence, or when placeholder zero-new-word readings bypass CI+1 intent.
</task>

<task id="04-02-2" title="Add staged theme rejection tests">
Add tests that prove early curriculum tiers reject work, health, money pressure, admin pressure, and serious adult logistics, and that language island unlocks do not occur before the practical threshold.
</task>

<task id="04-02-3" title="Verify and update planning evidence">
Run `npm.cmd test`, update `.planning/BASELINE.md` if counts or test totals change, and note any validator gaps that should become deferred issues instead of being folded into this plan.
</task>

## Success Criteria

- CI+1 reading validation catches bad coverage and excessive new-word load.
- Early tiers reject adult operating-life themes.
- Language islands remain gated at the intended thresholds.
- `npm.cmd test` passes.
